AI-DLC v1 Simulator

Follow one change to an identity risk product through all 11 stages of AI-DLC v1. You'll see what the AI reads, what it does, what people decide, what gets written into the project, and who has to approve before anything moves on.

How to play

  1. Pick a roleProduct Owner, Software Engineer or Project Manager.
  2. Press StartThe change begins its journey through the framework.
  3. Act when it's your turnGuess who approves each stage, answer your questions, open what the AI writes, then approve.
  4. Score pointsEarn points and badges for good habits. Lose points if you approve without reading.
Follow the prompts. The Your turn bar at the top tells you what to do next. The page scrolls you to the right place and outlines it.
Or explore at your own pace. Scroll around and open any file in Inputs and outputs or the Project folder before you answer. The AI waits for you, and reading first earns points.

The scenario

Spot identity details reused across many loan applications

The product

Identity Risk API. Lenders send us a loan applicant's details. We return a risk score from 0 to 100, plus reason codes that explain the score.

The problem

Fraudsters reuse one person's identity on many applications in a short time. Lenders say our score doesn't warn them about this.

The team

Priya (Product Owner), Sam (Software Engineer) and Jordan (Project Manager), working with an AI assistant that follows the AI-DLC v1 rules.

This scenario is illustrative: the product, people, times and figures are made up for training.
